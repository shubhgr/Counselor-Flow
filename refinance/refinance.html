Quick answers.

How to refinance student loans?

Share your current loan details, compare eligible lender offers, then complete the lender’s application and document process.

How much can I save by refinancing my education loan?

Your potential savings depend on the balance, interest rate, remaining term, fees and approved refinance offer. Use the calculator for an illustrative comparison.

Can I switch from an INR loan to a USD loan?

That depends on lender eligibility, residency and your financial profile. An expert can explain the options available for your situation.

Does refinancing affect my CIBIL or credit score?

Checking and applying may involve a lender credit inquiry. Ask the lender whether it is a soft or hard inquiry before proceeding.

What documents are required for education loan refinancing?

Requirements vary, but lenders commonly ask for identity, income, employment and current-loan documents.

Can I refinance if I’m still studying or on OPT/F-1?

Eligibility varies by lender, programme stage and income profile. Discuss your current status with an expert before applying.

Is Section 80E tax benefit available after refinancing?

Tax treatment depends on your circumstances and the loan structure. Consult a qualified tax professional for advice specific to you.

What is the minimum loan amount to refinance?

Each lender sets its own minimum balance and other eligibility requirements.

Can I remove my parents as cosigners through refinancing?

Some refinance products may allow a borrower to qualify without the original cosigner, subject to lender approval and eligibility.

What is the difference between refinance and balance transfer?

Both replace an existing loan, but the product structure, lender, currency and terms can differ. Review the full terms before choosing.

Which is better—refinancing with an Indian bank or a US lender?

The right option depends on eligibility, rate, currency, fees and your long-term plans. Compare approved offers side by side.

Can I refinance an SBI, Avanse or Credila education loan?

Eligibility depends on the receiving lender’s criteria and your loan, income and credit profile.
