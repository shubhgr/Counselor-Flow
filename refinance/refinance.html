Questions
before you
start?

What to know before taking the next step with your student loan.

01Who can refinance a student loan?

Eligibility depends on the lender and your loan, income and credit profile. An expert can help you understand which options may be available.

02Will it affect my credit score?

Ask the lender whether checking your options uses a soft or hard credit inquiry before you apply.

03What if my case is complex?

Share your situation with an expert so they can explain the documents and lender requirements relevant to your case.

04How long does the process take?

Timing varies by lender and depends on document verification and approval. Confirm the expected timeline before proceeding.
